| L1 | "Curfew" comes from Old/Anglo-French *covrefeu*, "cover the fire": an evening bell told households to cover or bank the hearth. | L2 | Ordinary evening light around 1500: tallow candles (animal fat; smoky, smelly) and rushlights (a dried rush dipped in fat).
