| M12 | Vienna General Hospital, two maternity clinics: the First trained doctors and medical students, the Second trained midwives. **Maternal mortality 1840-1846: 98.4 per 1,000 births in the First vs 36.2 in the Second (about 2.7 times)** (PMC3807776); in 1846 the First Clinic's mortality was "five times as high" as the Second's, and its annual rate fell from 11.4% in 1846 to 1.3% in 1848 (MJA 2018). After handwashing the First fell to 12.7 per 1,000 in 1848, close to the midwives' 13.3 (PMC3807776). Semmelweis (born 1818) was assistant to Prof. Klein at the First Clinic; April 1847 18.3%, June 2.2%, July 1.2%, August 1.9% (Wikipedia); his doctrine was "largely ignored, rejected, or ridiculed" (Braun, Virchow, others). **Re-check 2026-10-17: the "far less often with the midwives" claim is confirmed by two journal sources.
