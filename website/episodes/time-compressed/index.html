    Seven mornings

    1500 → 2100

    1. 1500 You wake up: A village It is midnight. Pitch black. And the whole village is awake.
    2. 1652 You wake up: London The air smells of smoke — and of something new.
    3. 1760 You wake up: A workshop Monday morning, and half the workshop is empty.
    4. 1883 You wake up: A small American town At midday the church clock strikes twelve. Then it is noon again.
    5. 1914 You wake up: Near Detroit The line of people looking for work stretches down the street.
    6. Today You wake up: Your bedroom Before your feet touch the floor, you have already checked your phone.
    7. 2100 Prediction · a scenario, not a fact That morning has not happened yet. But we can follow the evidence.
