    One experiment

    The story

    1. 1921 A university lab in Toronto Banting and Best start work in May. In November, Dog 33 begins the injections and is kept alive on them for seventy days.
    2. 11 Jan 1922 Toronto General Hospital Leonard Thompson, 14, gets the first injection. His blood sugar falls only a little.
    3. 23 Jan 1922 Toronto General Hospital Twelve days later, a cleaner extract. His blood sugar falls to normal.
    4. Today Everywhere 589 million adults live with diabetes, about 1 in 9, and more than 4 in 10 of them don’t know it.

    About this episode

    Medicine 2 of 10, a short explainer with one story and one mystery: a Toronto lab in 1921, a dog the lab called Dog 33, and Leonard Thompson, 14, whose first injection almost did nothing. Then the clue in the jar, what insulin actually does, why it isn’t a cure, the type 2 twist, and a one-dollar patent. This isn’t medical advice: if diabetes is part of your life, talk to a doctor who knows you.
