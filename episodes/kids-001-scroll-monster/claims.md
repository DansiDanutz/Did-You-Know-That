- **Corrections:** report an error by opening an issue at https://github.com/DansiDanutz/Did-You-Know-That/issues; corrections are dated in this ledger and reflected on the episode page.
- **Method:** I checked each claim in the narration and book against sources opened in this session, either through WebFetch or as PDFs downloaded and text-extracted with `pdftotext`. Primary sources were preferred: the survey reports themselves, WHO, AASM (via PMC), CDC, NCES and YouTube Help. Quotes are kept to about 12 words. "PDF p." means the page number in the PDF file, which can differ from the printed page number. A source is cited only if it was opened. Search-engine summaries were used to find URLs and are **not** cited as evidence.
- **Verdict key:** VERIFIED means the claim is accurate as said. NEEDS REWORDING means it is partly wrong, out of date or imprecise. UNVERIFIED means no source could be opened. HYPERBOLE means it is rhetoric, not a fact.

## Changes needed in the script

1. **K3 (update numbers):** Ages 2–4 should be "about **two** hours", based on 2:08 from the 2025 Common Sense census (2024 data). Ages 5–8 should be "about **three and a half** hours" (3:28). Cite the 2025 edition, not the 2020 one. Don't call it "for fun", because the total also counts a little homework and video-chatting.
2. **K4 ("scientists"):** Change to "researchers". Each survey had more than a thousand people, not "thousands of families": 1,306 kids and teens answered themselves (2021), and in separate surveys about 1,400–1,600 parents answered for young children. The sponsor is Common Sense Media, a nonprofit, and the polling was done by Ipsos.
3. **K7 (bedtime):** Say "kids who use screens at bedtime often sleep less and feel sleepier". The evidence is an association. Present "brain buzzing" as Dexter's metaphor, not as a proven mechanism.
4. **K8 (age band):** The WHO limit is for **ages 2–4** ("no more than 1 hour; less is better"), not 2–5. Credit it to the WHO. Drop the AAP "2–5, 1 hour" attribution, or re-verify it against the full AAP text before using it. The current AAP page stresses quality over a single fixed limit.
5. **K1/K2:** Add the year ("In 2021…") so the numbers are not presented as current.
6. **K9:** Don't claim autoplay is on or off by default for kids. Saying "a grown-up can switch it off" is accurate.
7. **K10:** Replace "nobody can stop kids from scrolling" with an empowering line (see the ledger).
